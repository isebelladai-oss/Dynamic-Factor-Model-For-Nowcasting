                         We construct a framework for measuring economic activity at high frequency, potentially in real time. We
                         use a variety of stock and flow data observed at mixed frequencies (including very high frequencies), and
                         we use a dynamic factor model that permits exact filtering. We illustrate the framework in a prototype
                         empirical example and a simulation study calibrated to the example.

                         KEY WORDS: Business cycle; Contraction; Dynamic factor model; Expansion; Macroeconomic fore
                                            casting; Recession; State space model; Turning point.
